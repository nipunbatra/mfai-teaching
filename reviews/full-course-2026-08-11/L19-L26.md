## Lecture notes

### L19 — Lagrange multipliers

- Replaced the circular maximum-entropy claim that positivity implies an interior optimum with an explicit search for an interior candidate. The later global argument is now signposted to L22.
- Cleaned the one-variable second-derivative check so the conclusion reads as a complete mathematical sentence.
- Residual low-confidence issue: the deck intentionally postpones the full maximum-entropy proof and inequality constraints; L20 and L22 supply those pieces.

### L20 — KKT conditions

- No source change was warranted. The worked active/inactive-constraint calculations and sign conventions are consistent throughout.
- Residual low-confidence issue: Slater's condition is presented operationally rather than proved. That level of detail is appropriate for this first KKT lecture.

### L21 — Linear and quadratic programming

- Replaced an incomplete edge-only argument for the LP vertex theorem with the correct convex-combination proof for a bounded polytope.
- Scoped all related geometry and summary claims to bounded polytopes, and aligned the checkpoint explanation with the corrected proof.
- Corrected the least-squares classification: affine equality or inequality constraints give a convex QP.
- Residual low-confidence issue: unbounded feasible sets are discussed through examples rather than a full theorem on extreme points and recession directions.

### L22 — Surprise and entropy

- Completed the maximum-entropy derivation: the Hessian establishes strict concavity in the simplex interior, and continuity handles boundary points, so the stationary point is the unique global maximizer.
- Residual low-confidence issue: the entropy axioms are motivated and stated, but their uniqueness theorem is not proved.

### L23 — Compression and Huffman coding

- Clarified that a decoder must recover symbol boundaries and that prefix-free codes are sufficient, not necessary, for unique decodability; added a concrete non-prefix uniquely decodable example.
- Rebuilt the prefix-tree figure so it exactly matches the five-symbol running code used in the surrounding slides.
- Residual low-confidence issue: Huffman optimality remains an exchange/induction proof sketch rather than a fully formal proof.

### L24 — Cross-entropy and KL divergence

- Repaired the Jensen proof for zero probabilities: it now treats infinite KL separately, sums on the support of `p`, and proves the equality conditions including the absence of mass outside that support.
- Split equality conditions onto a deliberate second derivation slide to keep the proof legible; this increases the deck from 55 to 56 pages.
- Residual low-confidence issue: mutual information is deliberately brief and optional.

### L25 — Markov chains

- Corrected the aperiodicity shortcut: a positive self-loop propagates aperiodicity to all states only in an irreducible chain.
- Residual low-confidence issue: convergence is explained visually and algebraically, but the general finite-state convergence theorem is not proved.

### L26 — Learning sequences

- Made the row-wise MLE derivation honest about its interior assumption.
- Added the KKT interpretation for zero transition counts and stated that a wholly unobserved row is not identifiable without a prior or other modelling choice.
- Qualified the summary formula to observed rows.
- Residual low-confidence issue: corpus-dependent n-gram statistics are generated by the lecture's reproducible figure script rather than re-derived on the slide.
